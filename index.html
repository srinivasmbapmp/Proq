What We Do

Four practices, one accountable partner

We don't hand off and disappear. Every engagement is backed by engineers who stay close to the outcome, not just the handover document.

01

Data Engineering & AI

This is where Proq Consultants has built its deepest expertise: architecting data platforms, integrating complex systems, and building applied AI on top of data you can actually trust. We build the pipelines, platforms, integrations and models that turn scattered systems into one trusted, decision-ready source of truth.

  • Modern data platforms (lakehouse & warehousing)
  • ETL / ELT pipeline engineering
  • Legacy-to-cloud systems integration (SI)
  • Master & reference data management
  • Applied AI & GenAI solution delivery
  • Predictive analytics & ML engineering
02

QA & AI Automation

Quality isn't a phase we bolt on at the end, it's engineered in from the start, and increasingly accelerated by AI. Our quality and automation practice covers everything from AI-assisted test generation to automated regression suites and performance benchmarking under real-world load, giving you the confidence to ship fast without ever shipping broken.

  • Test strategy & automation frameworks
  • AI-assisted test generation & automation
  • Performance & load testing
  • API & microservices test automation
  • CI/CD quality gates
  • UAT management & release sign-off
03

Cloud & Infrastructure

We design and run infrastructure that organisations can actually depend on, not just on launch day, but three years in. From lift-and-shift migrations to ground-up cloud-native rebuilds on AWS, Azure and GCP, every platform we build is engineered for resilience, observability and cost discipline from day one.

  • Cloud migration & modernisation
  • Multi-cloud & hybrid architecture
  • Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform, Bicep)
  • Cost optimisation & FinOps
  • Disaster recovery & resilience engineering
  • Managed cloud operations & monitoring
  • Cloud security, identity & compliance (ISO 27001, Essential Eight)
04

Staffing Services

Sometimes the fastest path forward isn't a project, it's the right people. We place experienced engineers directly into your team, or stand up a dedicated pod to own a workstream end to end, so you can scale delivery up or down without the overhead of a long hiring cycle.

  • Contract & contract-to-hire staffing
  • Dedicated engineering pods
  • Managed services & ongoing support
  • Product development teams
  • Flexible scaling, up or down
